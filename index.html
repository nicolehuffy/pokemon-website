Learn the basics (30 seconds)

What are types and weaknesses?

Every Pokémon and every attack has a type (Fire, Water, Grass…). Some types beat others: Water puts out Fire. If a Pokémon is weak to a type, attacks of that type do double (×2) or even quadruple (×4) damage. Resist means they take half. Immune means zero. A good team makes sure one enemy type can't beat everybody.

What are stats, natures and EVs?

Stats: HP (health), Attack/Defense (physical moves), Sp. Atk/Sp. Def (special moves), Speed (who moves first). Nature: a personality that raises one stat 10% and lowers another 10%. Choose one that boosts what your Pokémon is good at. EVs: bonus points you earn by battling. You can put up to 510 total, max 252 in one stat. Put them into the two stats your Pokémon uses most. Before Gen 3 games, EVs work a little differently, but the same plan is still a good guide.

What do "heavy hitter", "fast" and "tank" mean?

Heavy hitter: deals huge damage. Fast: usually moves first, so it hits before the enemy can. Tank: lots of HP and defense, so it soaks up attacks. A balanced team has a mix.
